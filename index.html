A developer slot runs about two hundred a day

Unlimited-request development is sold on volume. The unit actually priced is the active slot. Poket Dev delivers one active request at a time off a Trello board, so a month is the rental of one developer-sized slot: $5,995 on the monthly plan, which works out to $199.83 for each of the thirty days the slot answers. Onboarding takes about 24 hours to a live board and a 72-hour turnaround claim governs delivery, so the slot meters almost from the day it opens.

The ladder is a prepayment schedule

Quarterly billing drops the month to $5,495, and three months land at $16,485 against $17,985 billed monthly, keeping $1,500. The yearly rung sits at $4,995 a month, and the year closes at $59,940 instead of $71,940, a $12,000 trim of about a sixth. Requests stay unlimited on every rung, so the ladder never changes what can sit in the queue. It changes only when the money leaves.

The hire comparison survives the fine print

A fully loaded senior developer runs about $18,290 a month before roughly $13,500 of up-front hiring cost, which puts the first-year payroll line at $232,980. The subscription year on monthly billing is $71,940. The difference, $161,040, is the exact saving figure the site publishes. The yearly rung widens it to $173,040 kept. Cancel anytime and keep the code, so the arrangement can close mid-year whenever the backlog thins.

One slot sets the pace too

Pause a month and the meter pauses with it. Unused requests do not bank from month to month and no credit balance builds; a flat fee either runs or idles. A discount priced this way buys one thing, the operator's forward commitment, and the slot itself stays a per-day cost on every rung.
